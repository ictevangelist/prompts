Questions people ask

What are the Little Books of Generative AI Prompts?

They're four books of prompts I've written and tested for people who work in schools. Each prompt is ready to copy, paste and make your own in the AI tool your school has given you.

Who are the books for?

There's one for primary teachers, one for secondary teachers, one for school and trust leaders and one for support staff. Teaching assistants are covered by the two teacher books.

When will the prompts be here?

Soon. This is the 2027 edition, and the date is all to be revealed.
