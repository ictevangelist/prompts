Privacy notice

This site is run by Mark Anderson, trading as ICT Evangelist. In plain terms, here's what I do and don't collect.

What does this site collect about me?

Visit statistics, through Google Analytics, and nothing else. There are no adverts, no sign-up forms and no accounts, and I can't identify you from anything the site collects.

How does this site use Google Analytics?

I use Google Analytics to count visits and improve the site: how many people visit, which pages they read, and roughly where in the world they're reading from. It is used only for statistics. Nothing is used for advertising: advertising features are switched off and no data is shared for ads.

Google Analytics sets its own cookies on your device so that repeat visits can be counted. The data goes to Google, which processes it on my behalf. What I see is aggregated (page views, visit counts, general location, device type) and I can't identify you from it. Google Analytics 4 doesn't log or store visitors' IP addresses.

How do I turn analytics off?

Use the Turn off control at the bottom of every page. Your choice is saved in your browser's local storage, on your device only, and you can turn analytics back on in the same place. With JavaScript switched off, analytics never loads at all.

Who hosts the site?

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like every web host, GitHub keeps server logs that include visitors' IP addresses, for security and to keep the service running. I can't see those logs. GitHub's privacy statement explains what they do with them.

Are the fonts and images loaded from somewhere else?

No. The fonts, images and styles are all served from this site. The only thing loaded from elsewhere is the Google Analytics script described above.

What if I get in touch?

If you email me or use the contact form at ictevangelist.com, I'll use your details to reply and to plan any work we agree, and for nothing else.

How do I ask a question about this?

Email mark@ictevangelist.com. If you're unhappy with how I've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. If this notice changes, the date below will change with it.
